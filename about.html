What I work on

In my current job at Aqlix IT Solutions I plan and write SEO content and thought leadership. I use AI to draft faster and check every claim against its source before anything is published.

Outside work, AI Search Lab is where I test how AI tools pick which brands to name. Every test uses the same method: real buyer questions, each tool asked 3 times in a fresh chat, every brand and cited link logged. Published tests so far: ChatGPT vs Perplexity on 6 brands, Gemini vs ChatGPT, and Google's top 10 vs ChatGPT's sources.
