Questions people ask

What is AEO (answer engine optimization)?

AEO is the work of getting your business named and described correctly when people ask AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Mode for an answer or a recommendation.

How is AI SEO different from normal SEO?

SEO gets your page ranked. AI SEO gets your name into the answer. AI tools build answers from many pages, often directories, reviews and roundups rather than your own site, so the work covers what others say about you as well as your own pages. Good SEO is still the base.

Can you guarantee AI will recommend my business?

No. Nobody controls what an AI tool says, and anyone who promises it is guessing. What I can do is show you exactly what each tool says now, change what it reads, and retest the same questions every month so you can see what moved.
