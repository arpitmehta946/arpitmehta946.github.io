01 · Why it matters

Ranking on Google and being recommended by AI are now two different jobs.

AI tools don’t return ten blue links. They write one answer, name a few brands and cite a few pages. If your site isn’t one of those pages, the buyer may never see you, even if you rank well. And the answer changes from one run to the next, so a single screenshot tells you very little.
