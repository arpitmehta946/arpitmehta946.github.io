Do ChatGPT and Perplexity recommend the same Indian brands?

No. When I asked the same buying questions for 6 Indian brands, Perplexity named 5 of the 6 brands in every run. ChatGPT named only 1 of the 6 in every run.

Does being visible in Perplexity mean you're visible in ChatGPT?

No. In this test, Perplexity named 5 of the 6 Indian brands in all 3 runs. ChatGPT named 1 of the same 6 brands in all 3 runs, 2 brands in 1 of 3 runs, and 3 brands in none.

Check each AI tool separately. A good result in Perplexity told me nothing about ChatGPT.

Which brands did ChatGPT recommend instead?

ChatGPT recommended the biggest names in each category. Zoho (CRM), greytHR (payroll and HR software), Skechers (everyday shoes), Samsonite (cabin luggage) and Minimalist (acne skincare) were each named in all 3 ChatGPT runs for their category.

Was any brand missing from both ChatGPT and Perplexity?

Yes. The tested acne skincare brand was not named in any of the 6 answers (3 in ChatGPT, 3 in Perplexity). Minimalist and The Derma Co were named in all 6.

Is being named by ChatGPT the same as being recommended?

No. The CRM was the only tested brand ChatGPT named in all 3 runs. In a fuller check on 11 Nov 2026 across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Mode, the CRM came up in 11 of 12 answers.

In most of those answers the CRM was listed last and described as "the telecalling option", not as a general CRM for small businesses. ChatGPT never linked to the CRM's website.

How can I check if AI recommends my brand?

  1. Ask the question your customer asks, such as "best cabin luggage brand in India", not your brand name.
  2. Ask it 3 times, in a new chat each time. One answer is a screenshot, not a pattern.
  3. Check ChatGPT and Perplexity separately. In this test the two tools rarely agreed.
  4. Write down which brands are named instead of yours, and in what order.
  5. Open the pages the AI cites. In my earlier 10-search ChatGPT test, about half of the 134 links went to brands' and institutions' own home, product and pricing pages. Make your own product and pricing pages clear and specific.

How was this test run?

  • Tools: free ChatGPT and free Perplexity in Chrome, from India, on 11–12 Nov 2026.
  • Questions: one buyer question per brand, 6 questions in total (listed in the table above).
  • Runs: each question 3 times per tool, in a new chat each time. A brand counted as named if it appeared anywhere in the answer.
  • Brands: 6 Indian SaaS and direct-to-consumer brands, one per category, chosen before testing.

What are the limits of this test?

  • Small sample: 6 questions, 3 runs per tool, one location, two days. It shows a pattern, not a rule.
  • The CRM's ChatGPT runs came from the fuller 4-tool check on 11 Nov 2026. A separate set of 3 ChatGPT runs the same day, in a different account, named the CRM in only 1 of 3.
  • Free versions only: paid plans, chat history and other locations can change the answers.
  • Who ran it: my AI assistant typed and logged the runs in my accounts, following a fixed script.
