Same question. Different AI. Different winners.

I took 6 real Indian brands and asked ChatGPT and Perplexity the question their customers ask. 3 times each, in a new chat every time. The two tools rarely agreed on who to recommend.

By Arpit Sharma · Tested 11–12 Nov 2026 · Published 13 Nov 2026

What I found

Being visible in one AI tool tells you nothing about the others. Perplexity named 5 of the 6 brands in all 3 runs. ChatGPT named only 1 of them every time. It named 2 others in just 1 of 3 runs, and never named the last 3.

ChatGPT leaned on the biggest names. In every run it recommended the category leaders instead: Zoho, greytHR, Skechers, Samsonite and Minimalist each came up all 3 times in their category.

One brand was missing everywhere. The acne skincare brand wasn't named in any of the 6 answers. Minimalist and The Derma Co were named in all 6.

Named isn't the same as chosen

The CRM was the one brand ChatGPT named every time. When I ran a fuller check on it across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Mode (12 answers), it came up in 11. But it was usually named last, and framed as "the telecalling option" rather than a CRM for small businesses in general. And ChatGPT never linked to its website.

So it's worth checking where and how you're named, not just whether you are.

What I'd do with this

  1. Ask the question your customer asks, like "best cabin luggage brand in India", not your brand name.
  2. Run it 3 times in a new chat each time. One answer is a screenshot, not a pattern.
  3. Check ChatGPT and Perplexity separately. Doing well in one says nothing about the other.
  4. Note who gets named instead of you, and in what order.
  5. Look at the pages the AI cites. In my earlier tests, about half of ChatGPT's links went to brands' own product and pricing pages, so make yours clear and specific.

How I ran it

  • Tools: free ChatGPT and free Perplexity in Chrome, from India, 11–12 Nov 2026.
  • Runs: each question 3 times per tool, a new chat every time. A brand counted as named if it appeared anywhere in the answer.
  • Brands: 6 Indian SaaS and direct-to-consumer brands, one per category, picked before testing.

Limits

  • Small test: 6 questions, 3 runs per tool, one location, two days. It shows a pattern, not a law.
  • The CRM's ChatGPT runs came from the fuller 4-tool check on 11 Nov. A separate set of 3 runs on 11 Nov in a different ChatGPT account named it in only 1 of 3, which is another reason to test more than once.
  • Free versions only. Paid plans, logged-in history and other locations can change answers.
  • The runs were typed and logged by my AI assistant in my accounts, following a fixed script.
