# Arpit Sharma brand: Gulal

Decided 22 November 2026. Signature colour: **Gulal #C4245A**.

**Why Gulal:** love and strength in one colour. It's the Holi gulal of Vrindavan, deepened into a confident shade. Strength comes from how it's used: bold type, dark backgrounds for data, and strict consistency.

**Why this one:** no major SEO or AI tool owns it (Semrush and HubSpot use orange, LinkedIn uses blue, Yoast uses purple #A61E69). It's readable as text on white (contrast 5.6:1). The research behind it: Ehrenberg-Bass distinctive brand assets (uniqueness and consistency), Gorn et al. 1997 (lighter colours are liked more, brighter colours get more attention), and Labrecque & Milne 2012 (colour shapes brand personality).

## Fonts

- Headlines: **Bricolage Grotesque** (bold, slightly condensed)
- The voice line ("an engineer who writes"), quotes: **Newsreader italic**

## Rules

1. Gulal is the signature. About 10% of any image. Never a full Gulal background.
2. One key thing per image gets Gulal: the key number, the key word, or the line under the name.
3. Light mode for personal and story posts. Dark mode for tests, charts and the website.
4. Same layout every time: name in bold, "an engineer who writes" in Newsreader italic Gulal, short Gulal bar, one line under it.
5. Don't use orange, blue or teal as brand colours (they belong to the tools and platforms).
