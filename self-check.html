Does AI recommend your business?

Ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Mode the question your buyer asks. Ask each one 3 times. Count how often you are named. This page shows you how, and what the result means.

Before you start

Three rules that make the result trustworthy

New chat every time

Old chats and memory change the answer. Start fresh for every run.

Same words every time

Copy and paste the question. Small wording changes give different lists.

Ask like a buyer

Use a free or signed-out account where you can. That is what most of your buyers see.

Step 1

Write the question your buyer asks

Not your brand name. The question someone types before they know you exist. Three patterns work:

best [what you sell] for [who buys it] in [place]
[what you sell] for [a specific problem]
alternatives to [your biggest competitor]
Step 2

Ask 4 tools, 3 times each

Open each tool, paste the question, read the answer, then start a new chat and ask again. Twelve answers in total.

Step 4

What your result usually means

0of 12

AI has not connected you to this question. You are probably missing from the comparison and review pages it reads, and your own pages may not say clearly what you sell and who it is for.

1–5of 12

You are on the edge of the shortlist. Look at which tool skips you. Across 7 Indian brands I tested in Nov 2026, Perplexity often named challengers that ChatGPT left out.

6–9of 12

You are known, but check how you are described. In one test, a CRM came up in 11 of 12 answers yet was always listed last, as a niche tool.

10–12of 12

You are on the shortlist for this question. Now test the next 10 questions your buyers ask, and recheck monthly. Answers drift.
